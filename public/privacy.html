Estoria

Privacy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Estoria has no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and it never collects your writing. Your projects live on your own devices and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive.

Where your work is stored

  • In your browser. Projects, settings and pictures are saved in this browser's own storage on your device. They never leave it unless you choose one of the options below.
  • In a folder you pick (desktop Chrome and Edge). If you connect a folder on your computer, Estoria writes your project files and backups there.
  • In your Google Drive, if you connect it. Estoria writes your project files and backups to your own Drive so your other devices can open them.

Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, Estoria asks Google for two things:

  • Access only to files Estoria creates or that you pick for it (the drive.file permission). Estoria cannot see or open anything else in your Drive.
  • Your email address, used only to show which Google account is connected. It is kept in your browser and nowhere else.

Your projects travel straight from your browser to Google. They never pass through a server run by Estoria.

Estoria's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Estoria does not use your Google data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not share it with anyone.

The sign-in helper

To keep you signed in to Google Drive, Estoria uses a small sign-in helper at auth.labrarf.com. It exchanges Google's sign-in codes for access tokens and keeps one sign-in token in an encrypted cookie on your device.

  • It never sees your writing.
  • It stores nothing on its side: no database, no copies of your tokens.
  • It does not log sign-in codes or tokens.

That cookie exists only if you connect Google Drive, and it is the only cookie Estoria sets.

What Estoria does not do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of any data.
  • No account, password or profile.

Services the website relies on

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts load from Google Fonts. Like any web request, these receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads. They never receive your projects.
  • If you connect Google Drive, you sign in on Google's own page, so Estoria never sees your Google password. Choosing an existing folder or file opens Google's file picker, which loads from Google only when you open it.
  • The Estoria Android app opens the same project file through Android's own file picker and collects nothing.

Disconnecting and deleting

  • Disconnect Google Drive from inside Estoria, or remove Estoria's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Files Estoria wrote to your Drive stay yours. Delete them in Drive whenever you like.
  • To remove everything stored in this browser, clear the site's data in your browser settings. Export your projects first if you want to keep them.

Changes and contact
