Move a card by its grip

The tiles from the option B restyle, with two small additions: a grip to drag a card to a new place, and a position number you can type into. Everything below works. Drag by the ⠿, type a number and press Enter, or click a card anywhere else to open it.

  • Drag by the grip. The card lifts and follows the pointer. The others make room, and a dashed space shows where it will land.
  • Or type a position. Click the number, type 1, press Enter: the card moves to the top. This also works with only a keyboard, and on a touchscreen.
  • A click still opens the card. Pressing the grip or the number never opens it, even if you drop the card back where it was.
  • The order is saved in your file. It's the order the project already keeps, so the phone shows it after the next sync, with no phone update.
The tile

Where the grip and the number sit

  1. Grip ⠿, at the very start of the top row, before the badge. The only place a drag starts, so a click anywhere else always opens the card.
  2. Position number, at the end of the top row on character and world cards, where a chapter card shows its cast.
  3. Library and reference cards already show their position as the badge (01, 02, as a chapter shows its number), so there the badge itself is the number you type into. No second number.
  4. Keyboard: Tab to the grip and press the arrow keys to move the card one place at a time.
