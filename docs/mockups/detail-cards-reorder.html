Estoria · rearrange cards, to confirm

Move a card by its grip

The tiles from the option B restyle, with a grip to drag a card to a new place. Every place is shown with the drawer collapsed and expanded, and everything works: drag by the ⠿, or click a card anywhere else to open it. The two sizes of each drawer share one order, so moving a card in one moves it in the other.

  • Drag by the grip. The card lifts and follows the pointer, with a mouse or a finger. The others make room, and a dashed space shows where it will land.
  • Numbers while you rearrange. Press or Tab to a grip and every card shows its position. Type a number and press Enter to send a card straight there. Click away or press Escape and they hide.
  • A click still opens the card. Pressing the grip or the number never opens it, even if you drop the card back where it was.
  • The order is saved in your file. It's the order the project already keeps, so the phone shows it after the next sync, with no phone update.

Where the grip and the number sit

  1. Grip ⠿, at the very start of the top row, before the badge. The only place a drag starts, so a click anywhere else always opens the card.
  2. Position number on character and world cards, at the end of the top row (where a chapter card shows its cast). Hidden until you rearrange: pressing or focusing a grip shows it on every card in that list.
  3. Library and reference cards already show their position as the badge (01, 02, as a chapter shows its number), so there the badge itself is the number you type into. Nothing extra appears.
  4. Keyboard: Tab to the grip and press the arrow keys to move the card one place at a time.
