Anthology: a book of pieces that stay pieces

Expanding a piece into a collection today gives an ordinary book: numbered chapters, one kind and one line-break setting for everything. An anthology keeps each poem, story and essay as it is: its own kind, part names and line breaks, opening as its own page. The example is a made-up anthology of poems, stories and one essay. Everything is clickable. Items marked new are changes from today.
