How the cards look now

  • One line per card: a round avatar or a grey dot, the name, one small line under it.
  • Click the name or ▾ to open a card. You edit it right there in the list.
  • You can't see who someone is until you open the card.

Option A

Same look as a chapter card, opens in place

My recommendation
  • Looks like a chapter card. Badge, name in large serif type, two lines of description, count line at the bottom.
  • Opens in place. Click anywhere on a card and it opens right there in the list. Editing works exactly as it does today.
  • Readable at a glance. You can see who someone is without opening their card. The cards are taller, so fewer fit on screen.
  • Small change. Only the look of the cards changes.

Option B

Tiles like chapter cards, open in a window

  • Looks and acts like the board. Each card is a same-size tile. Click one and its details open in a window on top, the way a chapter opens.
  • A grid at full screen. The side panel is too narrow for two tiles, so there they stack in one column, like option A closed.
  • Editing moves. You edit in the window, not in the list.
  • Bigger change, and it overlaps with the later "rearrange the cards" work.
