Estoria · option B, to confirm

Every card looks and opens like a chapter card

This is option B applied everywhere: characters, world entries, the Notes library, a world entry's references and a chapter's pinned references. Each one is shown with the drawer collapsed (side panel) and expanded (full screen), and with a card clicked open. Scroll down.

  • One card design. Badge, name in serif type, two lines of text, a small count line at the bottom. The same as a chapter on the board.
  • Click to open. Clicking a card opens it in a window on top, like clicking a chapter. You edit there, not in the list.
  • Collapsed: cards stack in one column. Expanded: they sit in a grid.
  • Nothing in your file changes. This is only how things look and open, so the Android app and Drive are not affected.
