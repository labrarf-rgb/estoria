Characters and World tabs as tiles

An anthology piece's Characters and World tabs (a chapter uses the same tabs). Today on the left, tiles on the right, row by row. Scroll down.

  • Same tiles. The cast of this piece as character tiles, its world entries as world tiles. A grid, like the chapter's pinned references.
  • Click to open. Opens the same character or world window as the drawers do, so you can edit them from here.
  • Take one off this piece with "Remove from this piece" at the foot of the window. They stay in your cast. (Today that is the ✕ on the chip.)
  • Add with the "+ Add character" tile, which opens the same picker as today.
