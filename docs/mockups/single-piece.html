Single pieces in Estoria: mockups

A single piece is one work with no chapters: a short story, an essay, a poem, or anything else. It skips the chapter board and opens straight into what is now the chapter modal, laid out as a full page. Its kind is just a label: it changes what the parts are called, never which features you get. Items marked new are the changes.

The board is replaced by the piece itself. What sits inside a chapter modal today becomes the whole page: title, summary, kind, words and target, Idea / Draft / Done, the tabs, and the map filling the rest. Removed: New chapter, chapter numbers, Act, previous/next chapter arrows, Delete chapter, and Timeline. Board / Timeline becomes Map / Write. Versions stay. Connectors are always plain lines new in a single piece: no Therefore / But / And, and clicking one does nothing. They show order only. Switch the example above to see how the wording follows the kind.

About

Projects list: each single piece shows its kind as a label, and counts its parts by their own name ("7 scenes", "6 sections", "4 stanzas") instead of chapters. Single pieces can't be merged into a series. Change kind is safe at any time because the kind is only a label. Changing form (piece to book, book to piece, book to series) is on screens 5 to 9.

What happens
  • Prose: all chapters joined into one manuscript
  • Scenes: 19 scenes in one flow, laid out fresh
  • Connectors: become plain lines; Therefore / But / And labels are dropped
  • Cast, world, pins: everything any chapter used
  • Summaries and chapter notes: gathered into the piece's notes, under each chapter title
  • Target: the chapter targets added up
  • Dropped: acts, chapter statuses, the board layout, chapter links
  • Versions: every version is collapsed the same way

Cancel Turn into single piece

File › Turn into single piece, for any standalone book. Nothing is merged silently: the dialog says what joins, what's dropped, and that the whole book is saved first. Only what can't exist in a single piece is dropped (acts, board layout, connector labels), and the saved copy still has it. The kind defaults to the book's own kind if it has one.
